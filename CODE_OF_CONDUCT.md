# Code of conduct

Open-Q welcomes people of every background and experience level. Participation should be safe, respectful and focused on making useful audio software together.

## Working together

- Critique ideas and code constructively; treat the person behind them with care.
- Make room for questions, different perspectives and accessibility needs.
- Listen to feedback, acknowledge mistakes and help resolve disagreements calmly.

Discrimination, harassment, threats, sexual advances, personal attacks and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not expose someone's private information or retaliate against anyone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy covers Open-Q issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces, including conduct while officially representing Open-Q elsewhere.

## Reporting concerns

Contact maintainer **Yohan Senanayake** privately at [yohansenanayake4321@gmail.com](mailto:yohansenanayake4321@gmail.com). Describe what happened and include relevant links or screenshots where appropriate. Do not publish sensitive incident details in public issues.

Reports will be considered fairly and handled discreetly. Information will be shared only as needed to investigate and address the concern. Good-faith reports are welcome even when you are unsure whether this policy was violated.

## Moderation

The maintainer may remove harmful content, request changes in behaviour, issue warnings, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Responses should reflect the severity, impact and repetition of the behaviour. Serious abuse may require immediate removal. You may request clarification or reconsideration through the same private contact.

## Reference

This project-specific policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
